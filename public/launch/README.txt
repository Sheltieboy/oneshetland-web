OneShetland Launch Partner images
=================================

Everything in this folder is PUBLIC. Anyone on the internet can open it, so never
put private, confidential or unreleased files here.

How it works
- Give each partner its own folder, e.g. public/launch/heima/
- Use lowercase, web-safe names: letters, numbers and hyphens only, no spaces.
- Prefer jpg, jpeg, png or webp.

Example
  public/launch/heima/shop-front.jpg
  becomes
  https://oneshetland.com/launch/heima/shop-front.jpg
