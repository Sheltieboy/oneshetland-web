## Peerie Shop / Peerie Shop Café — what the records say
Three separate Directory records: **Peerie Shop** (shop, 01595 692816), **Peerie Shop Cafe** (01595 692817), **Peerie Isles** (01595 693202).
Peerie Shop's own site shows one address and website for shop and café, and says the café is "behind the Peerie Shop";
the Directory text for the shop says the same. But who runs the café today is not stated publicly (Shetland News, 2023, reports new
café operators), so the preview is bound to the **shop record only**, mentions the café only as the shop's own site does, and
claims nothing about its ownership. Peerie Isles is a different business at a different address and is not part of it.
Recommended: send the shop preview; ask in the first conversation who operates the café; if it is separate, give it its own light preview.

## Caveats
- Product prices are as read on 5 Oct 2026 (several from the store's public catalogue feed, not the rendered page); product images are
  hot-linked from each business's own Shopify CDN. Hero photographs are saved locally under `public/launch/<slug>/`.
- Jewellery product photographs on the store are 900px wide; only the workshop photograph is high resolution.
- The Dowry's site has no gallery; its hero uses the page's own soft-focus background photograph plus the illustrated logo from its OneShetland listing.
- Peerie Shop's site has no shopfront/interior photograph; its hero uses its homepage banner and the illustrated shopfront already on its listing.
- Da Craft Shed items are from the owner's Jade Crafts store, chosen to carry no third-party maker/brand name. Who made each item is not stated on its page.
- Soap story wording is the homepage's own (COPE Ltd; adults with learning disabilities and autism), attributed to the company's site.
- Directory data oddity (not changed): Peerie Shop's category is `services`; the preview treats it as retail.
