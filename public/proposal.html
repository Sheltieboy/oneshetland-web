A partnership proposal

Keeping Shetland's money, community and business in Shetland.

OneShetland is a community super-app for the islands — a single digital home for local businesses, events, payments, loyalty, delivery, jobs, dialect and heritage. Live on the web, and coming to iPhone and Android.

Everything Shetland, in one place — built for the islands, by the islands.

Made in Shetland · Darren Fullerton Consultancy Ltd · oneshetland.com

National apps take Shetland's spend, attention and data off the islands. OneShetland puts a digital high street and community hub in every resident's — and every visitor's — pocket, and keeps it here.

Shetland's businesses and communities are small, dispersed, and underserved by platforms built for cities. Getting online is costly; getting found is harder; the money that flows through a card machine mostly leaves.

OneShetland is the alternative that's owned in Shetland and reinvested in Shetland — one place where people find local, spend local, and stay connected to island life, and where every business has a free storefront and affordable tools.

Why it matters

What it does for Shetland

01Keeps money in Shetland

A local wallet, loyalty and cashback give residents and visitors a reason to spend with island businesses — and keep the margin here rather than with a national platform.

02Every business online, affordably

A free listing and low-cost tools bring the smallest, most remote firms into the digital high street — real digital inclusion, not a barrier.

03Stronger, connected communities

Hubs, notices and events knit dispersed communities together, while Spik and Auld Stories keep Shetland's dialect and heritage alive for the next generation.

04Answers island challenges

Local delivery, local hiring, a warm welcome for cruise visitors — plus first-party insight into how the island economy actually moves.

The ask

Where a partnership could take this

OneShetland is built and ready. What would multiply its impact is a trusted local partner to help it reach every business and household — and to join up with Shetland Islands Council on the things that serve the whole community.

  • A short conversationHalf an hour to walk you through it and explore where our aims overlap.
  • Introductions to businessesA trusted channel to help Shetland firms list and get set up.
  • Co-promotionReaching residents and visitors together — one island app, one message.
  • Working with the CouncilEvents, tourism and cruise welcome, town-centre and Shop-Local initiatives, shared island data.
  • Support for the public goodGuidance or funding for the heritage, dialect and digital-inclusion elements that benefit everyone, not just paying businesses.
  • A pilotA focused trial with a group of businesses or a community, measured together.
